# Data-analysis-for-Machine-learning-example
“Will a customer accept the coupon?” The goal of this project is to use visualizations and probability distributions to distinguish between customers who accepted a driving coupon versus those who did not. 

Through the data analysis, visualization and interpreting the results, my initial analysis is stated below. As a next step  deeper statistical study is needed for identifying significant factors.

Accepted coupon inference: The accepted coupons at about 56.84% were higher with Coffee House, Restaurant (<20), and Carry Out & Take away coupons. Bar coupons were least accepted with only 11.5% of all accepted coupons.
Drivers who accepted bar coupons are more likely to be frequent bar visitors, and their acceptance behavior may also 
be associated with age, passenger characteristics, and occupation and existing location. 
Acceptance rate overall is seen higher with younger or below 30 years drivers, frequently traveling alone or with friends, and coupons having a 1-day expiration, Sunny weather and 6 PM are popular times with destination as not urgent category.

Not Accepted coupon inference:
Drivers who did not accept coupons at about 43.1% tend to be associated with less frequent bar visits, driving alone or with children, older age groups 50+, short coupon expiration periods, unfavorable weather conditions, and 7am or 10pm of the day and on work destinations.
Non-acceptance is relatively high for Bar coupons, with approximately 59% compared to other coupons.
